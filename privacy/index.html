Where data is stored, and who else receives it

Your data is stored on our own servers in India. Only these service providers receive anything, and only what their service needs:

  • Meta (WhatsApp) receives your mobile number and the one-time code, to deliver it.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Push Notification service) receive your push address and a short notification, to deliver it. The notification on a locked screen never contains names, amounts or reasons.

The app contains no analytics or advertising tools. We may disclose data where required by law, or by a lawful order of a government agency or court.

How to delete your account and data

  • In the app: Profile → "Request deletion of my data".
  • Without the app: see https://legal.vidhiflow.com/delete, or email support@vidhiflow.com stating your registered mobile number; we will confirm it is you with a one-time code before deleting.
  • We act within 30 days. Signing out, or the end of your employment or partnership, removes your phone's access and push address at once. Records the law requires us to keep, such as employment and tax records, are kept only for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see a summary of your data, correct it, delete it (subject to the records the law requires us to keep), and withdraw your consent. You can nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can complain to our Grievance Officer (below) and, from when the duties under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act apply, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Sign-in uses a one-time code plus a device key kept in your phone's secure hardware. Access tokens last 5 minutes. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

The app is for employees and business partners only, and not for anyone under 18.
